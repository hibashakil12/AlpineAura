How to read this atlas

The four-tier risk scale

Every hazard is rated the way a mountain bulletin rates avalanche danger — by how likely it is to occur and how severe the consequences are if it does. Ratings are a general guide only; always check current local conditions and forecasts before you climb.

🟢 Low

Manageable with basic awareness. Unlikely to cause serious harm if reasonable precautions are followed.

🟡 Moderate

Requires active management — proper gear, technique, and route judgement meaningfully reduce the risk.

🟠 High

Serious consequences are plausible. Demands experience, planning, and constant reassessment on route.

🔴 Extreme

Life-threatening. Can escalate within minutes; margin for error is minimal even for experienced climbers.
