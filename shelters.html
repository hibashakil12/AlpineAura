UNDERSTANDING SHELTERS

Rest Beneath the Mountains

Shelters provide hikers with a safe place to rest, recover and stay protected during their mountain journey.

01

What Are Shelters?

Mountain shelters are safe resting places built along hiking routes where hikers can pause, sleep and protect themselves from harsh weather.

SAFE RESTING PLACES
02

Why Hikers Need Them

Long mountain journeys can be exhausting. Shelters give hikers a chance to rest, recover their energy and continue their journey safely.

REST & RECOVERY
03

Emergency Role

During sudden storms, injuries or extreme weather, shelters can provide temporary protection and become an important emergency refuge.

MOUNTAIN SAFETY

Stay Safe, Stay Prepared

Simple precautions can make every mountain journey safer, smarter and more enjoyable.

01

Read the Weather

Mountain weather can change quickly. Always check the forecast before heading out.

02

Know the Capacity

Make sure your chosen shelter has enough space before starting your journey.

03

Carry Emergency Gear

Keep first-aid, water, flashlight and essential emergency supplies with you.

04

Arrive Before Dark

Reach your shelter before nightfall to avoid navigating difficult terrain in darkness.

05

Share Your Route

Let someone you trust know your route, destination and expected arrival time.
