OUR STORY

Born From a Love For the Mountains

Alpine Aura was created for people who feel most alive surrounded by towering peaks, crisp mountain air and endless horizons.

From peaceful Himalayan valleys to challenging alpine trails, our mission is to inspire responsible exploration while helping adventurers discover the beauty, history and culture of mountain regions.
